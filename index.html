GOVERNMENT EXPERIMENT "ERRORMIND" SHUT DOWN AFTER SUBJECTS REPORT MEMORY LOSS, IDENTITY FRACTURE
BY STAFF REPORTER  |  DR. ELIAS VOSS

A classified cognitive research program operating under the codename "ERRORMIND PROTOCOL" has been forcibly suspended following disturbing reports from test subjects who claim to have lost the ability to distinguish between real and simulated experiences.

The program, which ran for 7 years at an undisclosed facility, reportedly used layered puzzle environments to "map the decision-making architecture of human subjects." Sources inside the program claim that the true objective was never cognitive mapping — it was identity erasure.

"We didn't realize what we were building," said one anonymous researcher. "By the time subjects reached the final layer, they no longer knew who they were before the experiment began. The protocol had replaced them."

Dr. Voss, lead architect of the protocol, could not be reached for comment. His office was found empty. His last known entry in the facility log reads: "The system is not testing them. The system IS them now."
